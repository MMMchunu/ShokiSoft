About The Company

Shoki Phaladi Manufacturing

Shoki Phaladi Manufacturing is a proudly South African company dedicated to producing reliable and affordable household hygiene products. Through our brand ShokiSoft, we focus on manufacturing toilet paper designed to meet the everyday hygiene needs of homes, student accommodations, retailers, and businesses.

Our Vision

To become a trusted South African manufacturer of hygiene products, known for reliable quality, affordable solutions, and dependable supply to homes, businesses, and institutions.

Our Mission

To manufacture dependable and affordable hygiene products that meet everyday needs while supporting local manufacturing and contributing to economic growth in South Africa.
